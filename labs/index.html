    OFFICIAL BRIEFING · HOW TO PLAY

    Welcome To Toot Match Labs

    Record authentic audio events, challenge friends to recreate them, and let our Institute settle the scoreboard scientifically.

    1
    🎯 Guess The Fart (Primary Blind Mode)

    See only the fart's funny name while the audio stays hidden. Record what you imagine it sounds like — then hear the dramatic reveal and see your score!

    2
    🦜 Mimic Mode (Play Fart First)

    Want to imitate it directly? Tap Play Fart First next to Guess to hear the fart upfront and flip the button into Mimic The Fart.

    3
    🎙️ Record New Fart & Multiplayer

    Sentry Auto Detect beeps when armed and auto-saves new Vault specimens. Switch Solo/Multiplayer anytime so the whole room locks guesses before reveal.

    4
    🔮 Blind Prediction Mode Beta

    Available anytime in ⚙️ Settings: record blind guesses before a live fart even happens, then capture the original fart afterward.
